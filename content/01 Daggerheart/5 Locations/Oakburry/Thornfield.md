## THORNFIELD

### Overview

Thornfield is a small farming village on the eastern edge of [[Oakbury]], the last settlement before the county border. It sits where three roads converge — the eastern road from beyond the county, a northwestern path that narrows into Duskwood toward Brackhollow, and a southwestern road that cuts across open fields toward the foothills and Cinderwick. Both western routes eventually lead to Greymill, the county seat, but there is no direct road from Thornfield.

It is not much to look at. A few dozen homesteads surrounded by fields of barley and turnips. Goat pastures on the hillside. A single inn. A stone chapel on the hill. A sawmill at the tree line. By all appearances, an unremarkable place — but as the first point of entry into [[Oakbury]] from the east, Thornfield sees every traveler, merchant, and stranger that the county road carries in. The troupe arrived here in [[S01E01 Arrival|Session 1]].

### Key Features

- **[[The Thorn and Thistle]]:** The village's only inn, run by [[Marta Selwyn]]. Serves as the social hub of Thornfield — if anything is happening in the village, word of it passes through here. A request board near the entrance posts work from locals looking for help.
- **[[Church of the Light]]:** A small stone chapel on the hill above the village. Simple, sturdy construction with no resident priest. [[Elder Tomek]] sometimes leads informal gatherings here, and someone keeps fresh wildflowers on the altar.
- **[[Hagen's Sawmill]]:** A working sawmill at the tree line where the village meets the edge of Duskwood, operated by [[Hagen Brauer]]. The constant smell of fresh-cut timber drifts downhill into the village. It is the last structure before the forest closes in.
- **The Crossroads:** Where the three roads meet at the village center — east toward the county border, northwest into Duskwood toward Brackhollow, and southwest toward Cinderwick. A worn signpost marks the junction.
- **Hillside Pastures:** Goat pastures on the rising ground north of the village, with a clear view of the surrounding fields and the dark edge of Duskwood beyond.

### Notable Inhabitants

- [[Elder Tomek]] — Village elder, a firbolg of about 125 years. Greeted the troupe on their arrival and pointed them toward the inn and the chapel.
- [[Marta Selwyn]] — Innkeeper of [[The Thorn and Thistle]]. Keeps the place running almost single-handedly.
- [[Hagen Brauer]] — Sawyer who operates [[Hagen's Sawmill]] at the Duskwood tree line. Speaks little, works constantly.

### Atmosphere & Senses

The air smells of cut timber and turned earth. Smoke rises from chimneys in thin grey threads. A dog barks somewhere between the homesteads. The fields stretch out flat and golden to the east, but to the northwest the tree line of Duskwood rises dark and close — a constant reminder that the wilderness is never far. At dusk, the forest edge swallows the last of the light while the village lanterns are still being lit.

### Hooks & Rumors

- The request board at [[The Thorn and Thistle]] is the most reliable source of local work — travelers and villagers alike post what they need done.
- Bandits have been reported on the road through Duskwood, making the route to Brackhollow and Greymill dangerous.
- The [[Church of the Light]] has gone without a proper cleric for some time. Some villagers worry what that means if real trouble comes.
- [[Hagen Brauer|Hagen]] works at the very edge of Duskwood and keeps a hand axe on his belt at all times — he knows better than most what moves among those trees.

### GM Notes

- **Travel connections:** East leads out of [[Oakbury]] (county border). Northwest through Duskwood reaches Brackhollow. Southwest across open fields reaches Cinderwick. Both western roads eventually connect to Greymill, but there is no direct route from Thornfield.
- **Starting location:** The troupe entered [[Oakbury]] through Thornfield. It serves as the campaign's initial base of operations.
- **Tone:** Quiet, pastoral, and slightly on edge. The village is safe but sits right at the boundary between settled farmland and the wild Duskwood. Encounters here should feel grounded — local problems, not epic threats.
