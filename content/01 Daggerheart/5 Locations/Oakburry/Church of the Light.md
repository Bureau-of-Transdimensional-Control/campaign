A small stone chapel on the hill above [[Thornfield]]. Simple construction — rough-cut stone walls, a peaked timber roof, narrow windows. No stained glass, no bell tower. Built to last, not to impress.

The church has no resident priest. Services are held when a traveling cleric passes through, which is not often. In the meantime, the villagers tend the building themselves. [[Elder Tomek]] sometimes leads informal gatherings here, though he makes no claim to holy authority.

## Notable Features

- One of the two landmarks [[Elder Tomek]] pointed the troupe toward when they arrived in [[Thornfield]].
- The interior is spare — wooden pews, a stone altar, a few candle sconces. Someone keeps fresh wildflowers on the altar.
- Sits on higher ground with a clear view of the village and the surrounding fields.
