A working sawmill at the tree line where [[Thornfield]] meets the edge of Duskwood. The constant smell of fresh-cut timber drifts downhill into the village.

Operated by [[Hagen Brauer]], a broad-shouldered firbolg who speaks little and works constantly. His hands are scarred from years of timber work, and sawdust clings to his brown fur like a second skin.

## Notable Features

- Sits right at the boundary between farmland and forest — the last structure before the trees close in.
- Supplies most of [[Thornfield]]'s building timber and firewood.
- [[Hagen Brauer|Hagen]] keeps a hand axe on his belt at all times — habit, not decoration. The edge of Duskwood is not a place to be unarmed.
