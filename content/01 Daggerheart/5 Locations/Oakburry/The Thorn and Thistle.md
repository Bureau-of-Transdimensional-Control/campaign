The only inn in [[Thornfield]] — a squat, timber-framed building with a thatched roof and a faded sign creaking above the door. It smells of woodsmoke, stew, and old ale.

Run by [[Marta Selwyn]], who keeps the place clean, the food honest, and the prices fair. She does most of the work herself.

## Notable Features

- **Request board** near the entrance — postings from locals looking for help. The troupe took both requests during their first visit.
- **Taproom** — a handful of rough tables, a stone hearth, and a bar. Nothing fancy, but warm and dry.
- The inn serves as [[Thornfield]]'s social hub — if anything is happening in the village, word of it passes through here.

## History

The troupe first visited during [[S01E01 Arrival|Session 1]], where they met [[Marta Selwyn|Marta]] and picked up work from the request board.
