## COUNTY OF OAKBURY

### Overview

Far from the great cities, in a quiet corner of the kingdom, lies Oakbury — a county of rolling fields, old forests, and small communities where life moves at the pace of the seasons. It is governed by [[Aldric Greybrand|Count Aldric Greybrand]], an aging faun of roughly 190 years who has held the seat for over a century and has learned that most problems resolve themselves if you wait long enough.

Oakbury is not a place where great events happen. Or so its people believe. The county has its share of troubles — bandits on the roads, beasts in the forest, the occasional strange sighting — but these are the ordinary problems of ordinary folk. The kind of problems that adventurers are made for.

### Key Features

- **Greymill (County Seat):** A modest but busy town on the banks of the Ashwater river, known for its mills, its market, and the local chapter of the Scroll & Feather — a kingdom-supported network of taverns that serves as a hub and meeting place for beginning adventurers. If you need work, information, or a warm meal, the Scroll & Feather is where you start. [[Aldric Greybrand|Count Greybrand]] resides here and holds court.
- **[[Thornfield]]:** A small farming village on the eastern edge of the county, where three roads converge. The last settlement before the county border and the troupe's point of arrival in [[S01E01 Arrival|Session 1]]. Home to [[The Thorn and Thistle]], the [[Church of the Light]], and [[Hagen's Sawmill]].
- **Brackhollow:** A settlement deeper in the woods, reached by the northwestern road out of [[Thornfield]] through Duskwood.
- **Cinderwick:** A village to the south, near old ruins that most people have the good sense to avoid. [[Aldric Greybrand|Count Greybrand's]] family estate — an old stone manor in the foothills — lies nearby.
- **Duskwood:** A great forest stretching across much of the county's interior. Ancient, dark, and home to things that prefer not to be disturbed. The road through Duskwood connects [[Thornfield]] to Brackhollow and eventually to Greymill.
- **Stillmere:** A lake to the west, known for its unusual calm. Beautiful and unsettling in equal measure.
- **The Ashwater:** The river that runs through Greymill, powering the town's mills and serving as a lifeline for trade.

### Notable Inhabitants

- [[Aldric Greybrand|Count Aldric Greybrand]] — Faun nobleman, county governor. Resides in Greymill but retreats increasingly to his family estate near Cinderwick.
- [[Elder Tomek]] — Village elder of [[Thornfield]].
- [[Marta Selwyn]] — Innkeeper of [[The Thorn and Thistle]] in [[Thornfield]].
- [[Hagen Brauer]] — Sawyer operating [[Hagen's Sawmill]] in [[Thornfield]].

### Atmosphere & Senses

Oakbury is a land of long horizons and old silences. The fields roll golden in late summer, broken by hedgerows and the occasional lone oak. The air carries the smell of hay, woodsmoke, and distant rain. To the east and south, the land is open and gentle. But step off the road toward the interior and the trees close in fast — Duskwood is never far, and its shadow falls long over the county's mood. Locals speak of the forest the way people speak of a neighbor they don't trust: politely, carefully, and never after dark.

### Hooks & Rumors

- The Scroll & Feather in Greymill is the county's clearinghouse for adventuring work — any job too big or too strange for locals ends up posted there.
- Bandits have grown bolder on the Duskwood roads, particularly between [[Thornfield]] and Brackhollow.
- The ruins near Cinderwick draw occasional treasure-seekers, few of whom come back with anything worth the trouble. Some don't come back at all.
- Stillmere has a reputation. Fishermen avoid its deeper waters, and the stories about what lies beneath change with every telling.
- [[Aldric Greybrand|Count Greybrand]] has governed for over a century and shows no sign of naming a successor. Some in Greymill wonder what happens when he finally steps aside — or doesn't.

### GM Notes

- **Scale:** Oakbury is a rural county, not a city-state. Travel between settlements takes hours or days on foot, not minutes. The roads are dirt, the infrastructure is sparse, and help is rarely close at hand.
- **Travel routes from Thornfield:** Northwest through Duskwood to Brackhollow. Southwest across open fields to Cinderwick. Both routes eventually reach Greymill, but there is no direct road from [[Thornfield]] to the county seat.
- **Tone:** Low fantasy, grounded. The dangers here are real but human-scaled — bandits, beasts, old ruins with teeth. The deeper mysteries (Duskwood, Stillmere, the ruins near Cinderwick) should unfold slowly.
- **Campaign scope:** The troupe entered Oakbury from the east, arriving in [[Thornfield]]. The county provides a contained sandbox with escalating threats radiating outward from the safe eastern farmland toward the wilder interior and south.
