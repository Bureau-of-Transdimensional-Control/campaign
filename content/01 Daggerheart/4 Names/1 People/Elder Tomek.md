## Appearance

Lean and stooped where most firbolgs are broad, with a long grey-furred face more bison than man. His left horn is cracked near the base — an old injury he never explains. Walks with a pronounced limp and a gnarled cane. His eyes are milky at the edges but sharpen when something catches his interest.

## Notes

- Greeted the troupe when they arrived in [[Thornfield]] during [[S01E01 Arrival|Session 1]].
- Pointed them toward [[The Thorn and Thistle]] and the [[Church of the Light]].
