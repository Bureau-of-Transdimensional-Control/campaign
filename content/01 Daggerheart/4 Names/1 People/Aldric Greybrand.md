## Appearance

Tall for his age but beginning to stoop, with the long, angular frame common to older fauns. His features have grown increasingly caprine with the years — a long face with a grey-white beard that blends seamlessly into the fur of his jaw, deep-set amber eyes with square pupils, and a pair of heavy, deeply ridged horns that curve back and down past his ears. His fur, once brown, has faded to the color of old ash.

He dresses plainly for a nobleman — dark wool and good leather, well-made but never showy. A silver clasp at his collar bears the Greybrand heraldry: a horn against a backdrop of forest, mountains, and lake. His hands are weathered and spotted, but his grip is still firm. He walks slowly, deliberately, with the careful economy of someone who knows his body won't forgive carelessness.

## Personality

His voice is low and unhurried. He listens longer than most expect a count to listen, and when he speaks, he tends to say less than people want to hear. Those who mistake his quiet for weakness rarely make the mistake twice. Those who mistake it for indifference are usually right — he has governed [[Oakbury]] for over a century and has learned that most problems resolve themselves if you wait long enough. The ones that don't, he handles personally.

## Notes

- Resides in Greymill, where he holds court and manages the county's affairs.
- His family estate — an old stone manor in the foothills near Cinderwick — is where he retreats when politics grow tiresome. The locals say he spends more time there with each passing year.
