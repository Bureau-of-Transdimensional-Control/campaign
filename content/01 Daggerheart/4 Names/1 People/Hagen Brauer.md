## Appearance

Broad-shouldered and ox-faced, with a heavy brow and short, curved horns. His brown fur is flecked with sawdust more often than not. Speaks little, but his hands — massive, scarred from years of timber work — are never still. Wears a thick leather vest and keeps a hand axe on his belt out of habit.

## Notes

- Operates the sawmill at the edge of Duskwood in [[Thornfield]].
