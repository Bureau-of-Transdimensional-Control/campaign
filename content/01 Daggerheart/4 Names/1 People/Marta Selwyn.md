## Appearance

A sturdy woman with sun-weathered skin and calloused hands. Her dark hair is streaked with grey and pulled back in a practical knot. Sharp brown eyes that miss nothing in her taproom. Wears a worn apron over simple clothes, sleeves always rolled up.

## Notes

- Met the troupe at [[The Thorn and Thistle]] during [[S01E01 Arrival|Session 1]].
- Hired them to deal with bandits on the road through Duskwood.
