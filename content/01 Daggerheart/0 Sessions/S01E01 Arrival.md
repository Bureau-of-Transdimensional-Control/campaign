**Players:** [[Nyx]] (Mattias), [[Mira]] (Maria), [[Clanker]] (Sidney), [[Gilbert]] (Iliya)

---

The troupe arrived in [[Thornfield]] from the east — a small farming village on the edge of [[Oakbury]], unremarkable in every way that mattered. They set up a quick performance in the village square to announce themselves.

[[Elder Tomek]], the village's old firbolg leader, came out to greet them. He walked with a limp and a cane and spoke slowly, but his eyes were sharp enough. He pointed them toward two places worth visiting: [[The Thorn and Thistle]], the village inn, and the [[Church of the Light|chapel]] on the hill.

The group chose the inn. [[Nyx]] threw [[Gilbert]] through the front door — an introduction of sorts. Inside they met [[Marta Selwyn]], the innkeeper, a no-nonsense woman who'd clearly seen worse. The inn had a request board near the entrance, and the troupe took both postings from it.

They decided to help [[Marta Selwyn|Marta]] first: bandits had been harassing travelers on the road through Duskwood. The group headed into the forest, tracked the bandits down without much trouble, and made short work of them. A few were killed, the rest scattered into the trees.

The troupe returned to [[The Thorn and Thistle]] and collected their reward from Marta. A decent start.
